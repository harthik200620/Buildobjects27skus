# Tiles (TIL) — SKU selection

Category `tiles`, code `TIL`, unit `box`, GST 18%, HSN 6907.
Governing standards: **IS 15622** (pressed ceramic tiles), **ISO 13006** (classification, Group BIa)
with **ISO 10545** as the test-method series.
Selected 2026-08-22 for the Andhra Pradesh / Telangana market.

The flagship format for all three brands is the **600 × 1200 mm (2 × 4 ft) rectified,
polished glazed/vitrified floor tile** — the size an AP/Telangana buyer specifies for a
continuous marble-look floor. All three picks are that format, which also makes them
directly comparable on the PDP compare engine.

---

## Kajaria — `kajariaceramics.com`

**Domain confirmed as manufacturer.** The site is Kajaria Ceramics Ltd's own corporate and
product site: it carries the investor section (Buyback 2026, Board composition), the
manufacturing-facilities pages, and the About page stating 87.80 million sq. metres of
annual capacity across nine plants — including Srikalahasti (Andhra Pradesh) and Balanagar
(Telangana). Not a marketplace.

- **SKU chosen:** Venato Bianco, model **GP00215** — glazed vitrified tile, 60 × 120 cm, Polish finish, white
- **URL:** https://www.kajariaceramics.com/products/venato-bianco
- **Why this one:** It is on Kajaria's own 60 × 120 cm glazed-vitrified listing (filtered
  `size_ids[]=83`), and it is the best-documented of that set: the product page states the
  category, the exact size, the finish, the colour, both floor and wall application, the
  factory SKU code, and the specific Gresbond adhesive grades Kajaria recommends
  (VX-1 / VX-2 internal, EX-5 external). It also carries a full six-image gallery plus a
  room concept picture keyed to this design — more official imagery than any neighbouring
  design in the range. White veined marble in 600 × 1200 is Kajaria's highest-volume
  large-format look in South India.

## Somany — `somanyceramics.com`

**Domain confirmed as manufacturer.** Organization JSON-LD on the About page gives
`legalName: Somany Ceramics Limited`, `foundingDate: 1968`, `tickerSymbol: NSE:SOMANYCERA`
and the Noida registered address, with the plant timeline (Kassar 1971, Kadi 1983,
Duragres GVT line 2010). Not a marketplace.

- **SKU chosen:** GRANDE EL SEDONA CREMA FP, article **T31F119001859102** — Duragres GVT, FULL POLISHED, 600 × 1200 mm
- **URL:** https://www.somanyceramics.com/products/tiles/grande-el-sedona-crema-fp-t31f119001859102
- **Why this one:** Duragres is Somany's flagship glazed-vitrified brand — the line that
  earned the industry's first BIS mark on GVT in 2010 — and Grande is its 600 × 1200 mm
  large-format sub-range. This article is the best-documented one on the site: its
  Product JSON-LD names the collection (DURAGRES), category (GVT), the exact finish and
  size, the rooms it is meant for, the availability state, and four official 1200 × 600 px
  product images on Somany's own DAM. Crema (cream marble) is the highest-moving shade in
  the AP/Telangana retail counter.

## Johnson / H&R Johnson — `hrjohnsonindia.com`

**Domain confirmed as manufacturer.** The About page identifies H & R Johnson (India),
established 1958, as the tile division of **Prism Johnson Limited** (alongside Prism Cement
and Prism RMC), with its own CTDC ceramics R&D department and company-owned experience
centres including Hyderabad. Not a marketplace.

- **SKU chosen:** Floral Crema, article **YK1FLCR000000PJ** — Marbonite by Johnson, Elite DSF RT24, 60 × 120 cm, Polished
- **URL:** https://www.hrjohnsonindia.com/product-elite-dsf-rt24-floral-crema-yk1flcr000000pj
- **Why this one:** Marbonite is Johnson's flagship fully-vitrified tile brand and Elite
  DSF RT24 is its rectified 600 × 1200 mm collection. This product page is the most
  specification-complete of Johnson's large formats: it states size and pack (60 × 120 cm,
  2 tiles/box), coverage area per box (1.44 sq. mtr), finish (Polished), material
  (Vitrified tiles), collection, look and application (Floor & Wall) as discrete fields,
  and it carries the StainFree series description. Coverage per box is a *published*
  number here, which neither Kajaria nor Somany state online.

## Known gaps (carried into the SKU files as `ai_filled`)

None of the three brands publish per-design technical data online — thickness, water
absorption, breaking strength, MOR, MOHS, PEI, slip class and box weight are absent from
all three product pages (Johnson explicitly prints "Thickness: NA"). Those values are
`ai_filled` at confidence ≤ 0.7 from the IS 15622 / ISO 13006 Group BIa requirements for a
9 mm dry-pressed porcelain tile, and are flagged for replacement when a per-design
datasheet is obtained from the dealer. No BIS licence, CM/L or test-report number has been
recorded anywhere, because none was published.

Prices are `estimated`: none of the three sites publish MRP or a per-sqft rate.
