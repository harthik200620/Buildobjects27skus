# Epoxy (EPX) — brand and SKU selection

Category slug `epoxy`, code `EPX`, unit `kit`, GST 18 %.
Selected 2026-08-22. Three brands, one flagship construction-epoxy SKU each.

---

## 1. Sika — `ind.sika.com`

**Domain confirmed as manufacturer.** `ind.sika.com` is the India country site of Sika AG
(Baar, Switzerland, founded 1910). The product data sheet served from that domain carries the
Sika India Pvt. Ltd. address (620 Diamond Harbour Road, Kolkata 700 034) in its legal block, so
this is the manufacturer's own India site, not a marketplace.

- **SKU chosen:** Sikadur®-31 IN — 2-part thixotropic structural epoxy adhesive, 6 kg pre-batched unit
- **SKU code:** `EPX-SIK-SIKADUR31IN`
- **Official product URL:** https://ind.sika.com/en/construction/concrete-repair/bonding-primers/sikadur-31-in.html
- **India PDS:** https://ind.sika.com/dam/dms/in01/i/sikadur_-31_in.pdf (Dec 2019, v01.01, ref 020204030010000266)

**Why this is the flagship.** Sikadur-31 is the epoxy every Indian structural-repair contractor
knows by name, and `-31 IN` is the India-specific formulation (rated to +40 °C rather than the
global CF Normal's +30 °C, which matters in AP/Telangana). It is the only Sikadur variant on the
India site with a complete four-page India PDS: mix ratio, pot life, layer thickness, sag flow,
compressive/flexural/tensile/adhesion strengths and consumption are all published. It is also the
broadest-use product in the range — structural adhesive, repair mortar, joint filler and crack
sealer in one pack — so it anchors the category better than a single-purpose primer or grout.
The 6 kg unit (Part A 4 kg + Part B 2 kg) is the standard jobsite pack.

## 2. Fosroc — `fosroc.com`

**Domain confirmed as manufacturer.** `fosroc.com` is the corporate site of Fosroc International
(founded 1972, Dubai HQ; acquired by Saint-Gobain on 2 February 2025). India operations run through
Fosroc Chemicals (India) Pvt. Ltd., Embassy Point, Infantry Road, Bangalore 560 001 — the address
printed on the India TDS served from that domain. Not a marketplace.

- **SKU chosen:** Conbextra® EP10 — high-strength solvent-free epoxy resin free-flow grout, 1 litre pack
- **SKU code:** `EPX-FOS-CONBEXTRAEP10`
- **Official product URL:** https://www.fosroc.com/product/show/conbextra-ep10
- **India TDS:** https://www.fosroc.com/assets/productDatasheets/TDS-Conbextra-EP10-India.pdf (ref INDIA/2020/0314/A)

**Why this is the flagship.** Conbextra is Fosroc's best-known grout family in India, and EP10 is
its epoxy member — the one specified by name in tender documents ("the epoxy grout shall be
Conbextra EP10 supplied by Fosroc… a 7 day compressive strength of at least 80 N/mm²"). It is
dual-purpose: free-flow base-plate grouting from 0.25 to 10 mm, and low-viscosity crack/honeycomb
injection. The India TDS is the most numerically complete of the Fosroc epoxy range (pot life,
density, 1/3/7-day compressive, tensile and flexural strengths, EN 1504 classes, shelf life), and
it is the only one that publishes an EN 1504-3 Class R4 / EN 1504-6 declaration.

## 3. Pidilite (Dr. Fixit) — `drfixit.co.in`

**Domain confirmed as manufacturer.** `drfixit.co.in` is the Construction Chemicals Division site of
Pidilite Industries Limited (founded 1959 by Balvant Parekh; Ramkrishna Mandir Road, Andheri East,
Mumbai 400059) — the address printed on the technical data sheet served from that domain. Dr. Fixit
is a Pidilite house brand, not a reseller.

- **SKU chosen:** Dr. Fixit Epoxy Injection Grout (product 206) — two-component low-viscosity moisture-insensitive injection grout, 3 kg pack
- **SKU code:** `EPX-PID-EPOXYINJGROUT`
- **Official product URL / TDS:** https://www.drfixit.co.in/resources/library/technical-data/large-projects/206-dr-fixit-epoxy-injection-grout (rev 206-26/02/22)

**Why this is the flagship.** Dr. Fixit's consumer-facing catalogue is waterproofing-led; the
epoxies live in the Large Projects technical library, and product 206 is the best-documented one
there — a full properties table with mix ratio 100:60, specific gravity, pot life, Brookfield
viscosity, and BS 6319 Part 2/3/7 compressive, flexural and tensile results plus ASTM C 881 water
permeability. It is also the Pidilite epoxy an AP/Telangana buyer actually orders: structural crack
injection in slabs, walls, tanks and sea walls. (Fevitite, the alternative, is Pidilite's general
industrial/DIY epoxy adhesive with a much thinner published spec.)

## Notes on sources and gaps

- No brand publishes an MRP for these products. All three prices are `estimated` from distributor
  quote ranges seen on IndiaMART / TradeIndia in August 2026; each SKU file carries the basis in
  `price.note`.
- Amazon.in (`B097TWV3WR`, Fosroc) returned HTTP 500 and Flipkart (Dr. Fixit 3 kg) returned HTTP 403,
  so neither could be opened for a `fetched` price.
- `ind.sika.com` serves a "no image available" placeholder for Sikadur-31 IN, so the Sika hero and
  angle images come from an IndiaMART distributor listing and the pack shot from Sika India's own
  Shopify storefront.
- None of the three products declares a BIS/ISI licence; conformity is to ASTM C 881 (Sika,
  Dr. Fixit), BS EN 1504-3/-6 (Fosroc) and BS 6319 (Dr. Fixit). No licence or test-report numbers
  have been recorded anywhere in these files.
