Icons — 24px grid, 1.75 stroke, one chamfer, one accent

Butt caps and mitre joins, a 4.6-unit chamfer on the top-right of every container, and exactly one element per glyph in the accent. Rounded corners are what every icon pack does; a chamfer is what a drafting pen does.
