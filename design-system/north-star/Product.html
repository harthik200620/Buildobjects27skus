PPC replaces part of the clinker with fly ash. It gains strength more slowly than OPC 53 in the first week and ends up denser and less permeable, which is why it is the default for foundations, plastering and mass concrete in coastal Andhra — and why it wants a longer cure. Plan 10 days of curing, not seven.
