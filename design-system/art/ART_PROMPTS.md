# Backplate prompts

The exact prompts behind `fetch-art.mjs`, so any of them can be regenerated or
retuned. Every one shares the same grade so the set reads as one shoot:

> Colour grade: deep teal-cyan shadows crushed toward hex 06181D, warm amber
> highlights only from practical light, no blue-grey midtones. Anamorphic,
> shallow depth of field, fine film grain. Photorealistic. No lettering, no
> logos, no readable brand names, no people.

## Generated

**site-materials** (16:9) — Cinematic close photograph of stacked cement bags and
bundled steel TMT rebar on an Indian construction site at golden hour, a heap of
river sand beside them, a bamboo scaffolding leg entering frame. Dust motes in a
low raking sunbeam. Large empty negative space in the upper third. 50mm, f/2.0.

**catalogue-aisle** (16:9) — Looking down a long dark warehouse aisle stacked
high with Indian building materials on both sides — cement bags, tile cartons,
coils of wire, bundles of PVC pipe, paint drums — receding into darkness, lit
only by overhead lamps casting warm amber pools. Volumetric haze, strong
one-point perspective vanishing into black. 35mm, f/2.8.

**construct-frame** (16:9) — Golden hour, an RCC framed structure under
construction in India: raw grey concrete columns and beams, a freshly cast
first-floor slab with shuttering props beneath it, exposed rebar starter bars,
bamboo scaffolding, a blue tarpaulin, against a teal dusk sky. 35mm, f/2.8.

**blueprint-field** (1:1) — A seamless tileable technical drafting field on a
near-black teal ground: a faint 1px cyan grid at two scales plus sparse drafting
marks — dimension arrow terminators, section cut symbols, centre-line dashes,
detail bubbles — distributed so the tile repeats without a seam. Flat vector
look, ~8% opacity, no lettering or numerals.

**coins-field** (16:9) — Faint concentric guide arcs and radial tick marks in
amber hex F5B40A at very low opacity, like a machined dial face or a mint die
layout, with a soft warm amber radial bloom at the centre falling to black.

**bo-coin-hero** (1:1) — A thick circular gold coin at three-quarter angle,
brushed and polished amber gold hex F5B40A, an embossed interlocked B-O
monogram, deeply milled reeded edge, one soft key from upper left and a cool
cyan rim from behind right. Macro 100mm, f/5.6.

**bo-coin-edge** (1:1) — The same coin almost edge-on, tilted ~15°, so the
milled edge and real thickness dominate and the face is a foreshortened sliver.
